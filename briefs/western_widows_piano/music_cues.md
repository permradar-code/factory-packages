# Music cues: "The Widow's Piano"

All underscore cues are **instrumental**: the narrator speaks over almost all of them, and vocals under a voice kill intelligibility. The only vocal track is the optional end-credits song (M14).
Durations are targets; generators often ignore them, so the montage trims, loops and crossfades. Mix: underscore sits ~18–22 dB under the narrator, rises in clip gaps and on title cards.

Common tail for every prompt: *"Instrumental only, no vocals, no choir. Warm analog recording, intimate, cinematic western film score, 1870s American frontier feel, no modern drums, no synths, no electric guitar."*

## Prompts

**M01 — The Auction.** Slow, tense western underscore at 70 BPM. A lone resonator (dobro) guitar plays sparse bent notes over a low sustained cello drone and a soft ticking rim click like a pocket watch. Quiet murmuring tension that slowly tightens, then cuts to near silence and one held high violin note. Feeling of public humiliation and a held breath.

**M02 — Main Theme.** A simple, memorable main theme for a western drama, 84 BPM, in D major. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar introduces the melody, a solo fiddle answers, soft upright bass underneath. Nostalgic, warm, a little sad, like an old photograph on a shelf.

**M03 — Before the Drought.** Gentle melancholic underscore, 76 BPM. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar and soft sustained fiddle, a distant harmonica phrase every so often, very low dynamics so a narrator can speak over it. Memories of a good life slowly slipping away. Sparse, no sudden changes.

**M04 — The Empty Parlor.** Very slow solo piano, 60 BPM, minor key. Few notes, long silences between phrases, slightly out-of-tune old upright piano sound with soft felt. Grief in an empty room at night.

**M05 — A Hot Supper.** Light hopeful homestead music, 92 BPM. Clawhammer banjo and acoustic guitar with a gentle fiddle melody, upright bass, soft brushed snare. Morning work on a ranch, fresh start, quiet warmth, a child following a stranger around. Keep it soft enough to talk over.

**M06 — Shenandoah (piano).** Slow solo upright piano in a log-cabin parlor playing the traditional American folk melody "Shenandoah", 66 BPM, simple hymn-like arrangement, gentle rubato, warm and tender, a little imperfect like a woman playing for herself at night.
*Note: generators may not reproduce the exact traditional tune. If it comes out as "something else", the montage chat can render the public-domain melody from notes instead.*

**M07 — The Ridge.** Quiet mysterious underscore, 70 BPM. Low tremolo strings, sparse plucked guitar harmonics, wind-like sustained violin, a slow rising tension. Discovery of a secret, something valuable and dangerous.

**M08 — Mr. Pike.** Dark, slow villain theme, 64 BPM, minor key. Low pizzicato double bass walking like footsteps, a muted cello line, a cold sustained harmonium chord. Polite menace. Second half thins out to a lonely solo violin.

**M09 — Fire in the Night.** Urgent western action underscore, 132 BPM. Driving low tom drums like galloping hooves, tremolo strings, sharp fiddle stabs, rising danger for 70 seconds; then the drums stop and a soft exhausted guitar and cello play a calm dawn ending.

**M10 — Gone.** Very sad, lonely underscore, 62 BPM. A solo harmonica plays a slow mournful line over sparse fingerpicked guitar and a low cello. Empty road, a woman waiting at a gate, a child at a window. Long pauses.

**M11 — Saturday.** Tense build into triumph, starting at 70 BPM: a low cello drone and ticking pulse (callback to the auction), slowly gathering; then galloping drums and full strings, fiddle and acoustic guitars burst into a heroic western theme in D major as riders arrive; ends on a strong resolved chord.

**M12 — The Letter.** Emotional revelation, 68 BPM. Solo piano begins softly, a cello joins, then warm strings swell; in the middle a quiet military snare roll and distant bugle-like horn hint at an 1862 battlefield memory; ends tender and tearful on solo piano.

**M13 — Home.** Warm resolution of the main theme, 84 BPM, D major. Fingerpicked guitar, fiddle melody, upright bass, soft strings rising underneath, a quiet piano echoing the melody. Sunset, a lamp in the window, a family finally safe. Gentle fade-out.

**M14 — End song (optional, the only cue with vocals).** Slow country-folk ballad, 78 BPM, female vocalist with a warm, slightly husky Americana voice, acoustic guitar, fiddle, pedal steel, upright bass. Original lyrics about a widow's old piano, a quiet stranger who kept a promise to a fallen friend, and a light left burning in the window. Heartfelt, simple, radio-friendly classic country sound.
