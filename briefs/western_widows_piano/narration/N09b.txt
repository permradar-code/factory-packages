They were married the following autumn, when the aspens turned gold again, in the little church at the end of Main Street.

Lily walked ahead of her mother, scattering wildflowers, and half of Cedar Bluff came to watch.

This time, nobody laughed.

And that night, in the house with the light in the window, Clara played Shenandoah, and John Mercer sang along. Badly. And with all his heart.
