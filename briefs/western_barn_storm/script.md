# Tales of Cedar Bluff — Film 3: THE BARN IN THE STORM

Рабочий сценарий v1 (8 окт 2026). Длина ~18–19 мин. Бюджет генерации ≤ $70.
Реплики героев — на английском (так будет в фильме), пояснения — по-русски.

**Время и место:** весна 1881 года, Колорадо (штат с 1876 года). Ранчо Харлана в долине под Сидар-Блаффом.
Весна выбрана нарочно: зелёная трава, полевые цветы, яркие дни между грозами. Ночью и в дождь идёт только ~¼ фильма.

**YouTube-название (основное):**
*A Widowed Rancher Let a Mother and Her Baby Sleep in His Barn — Three Days Later, Three Men Came for the Child*

**Превью:** ворота сарая под ливнем, тёплый свет фонаря на лице мокрой матери с младенцем, девочка держится за её юбку, в дверях высокий силуэт. Надпись: **MAY WE SLEEP HERE?**

### СЦЕНА 1. Ночь в доме
*Кухня, лампа, дождь за окном. Уэйд растапливает печь. Элли не снимает шаль.*

- **N01:** "The house had been quiet for three years. Quiet enough that Wade could hear the clock in the hall, and every board that creaked under a woman's weight, and the small, wet breathing of a baby who had cried himself to sleep."
- **WADE:** "There's a bed upstairs. Clean sheets."
- **ELLIE:** "We'll be gone before light, Mr…"
- **WADE:** "Harlan. And you'll be gone when the rain's gone. Not before."
- **MOLLY** (у печки, грея руки): "Is this your house all by yourself?"
- **WADE** (пауза): "It is now."
- *Ночь. Элли спит в кресле у двери, младенец на руках. Из-под шали видна рукоять маленького револьвера.* Уэйд замечает, молча накрывает её одеялом и уходит.
- **N02:** "She slept sitting up, between her children and the door. Wade knew that kind of sleep. He had slept like that himself, a long time ago, in a country where the next knock on the door could be the last."

### СЦЕНА 2. Утро. Колыбель
*Яркое весеннее утро, солнце после грозы, капли на траве.*

- Уэйд выходит из сарая и замирает: из трубы дым, на крыльце пахнет хлебом. Элли у плиты, Молли кормит кур.
- **WADE:** "You didn't have to do that."
- **ELLIE:** "I don't take charity, Mr. Harlan. I pay with what I have."
- **WADE:** "Then you're a rich woman, ma'am. Nobody's cooked in that kitchen in three years."
- *Молли лезет на сеновал за котёнком и находит под мешковиной резную колыбель.*
- **MOLLY:** "Mister! There's a baby bed up here! Whose is it?"
- *Уэйд у лестницы. Долгое молчание.*
- **WADE:** "Leave it be, Molly." (тише) "Please."
- **N03:** "Her name had been Anna. The boy had been called Daniel, after Wade's father. The fever came in the spring of '78 and took them both in a single week. Wade carved the cradle himself, the winter before the boy was born. After the funeral he carried it up to the loft, covered it with a feed sack, and never climbed that ladder again."
- *Ночью Элли тихо поднимается на сеновал и долго смотрит на колыбель.*

### СЦЕНА 2б. Крыльцо (ночь, ~1:20)
*Дождь стих, крыльцо, лампа, сверчки. Элли выносит ему кофе.*

- **ELLIE:** "She must have been very loved. Your wife."
- **WADE:** "Anna." (пауза) "She'd have had you out of those wet clothes and into hers before you got through the door. And she'd have given me hell for making you ask about the barn."
- **ELLIE:** "You didn't make me ask."
- **WADE:** "I made you stand in the rain long enough to ask."
- **ELLIE:** "My husband would have liked you, Mr. Harlan."
- **WADE:** "Wade."
- **N03b:** "Henry Vance had been nothing like his father. He had married a schoolteacher's daughter against the old man's orders, and worked his own shifts in his father's mine to prove he could. He wrote poems on the backs of assay slips, and was teaching Molly her letters, and was going to buy a little farm in the valley when the boy was old enough to ride. Then, one Tuesday morning, a beam gave way four hundred feet underground."

### СЦЕНА 2в. Дни на ранчо (монтаж, ~1:30)
*Солнечные дни. Молли ходит за Уэйдом хвостом: к колодцу, к коровнику, к забору.*

- **N02b:** "The rain came and went for days, one storm after another rolling over the ridge. And somewhere in those days the house began to sound different. A spoon against a pot. A little girl's boots on the stairs. A baby who laughed when the rooster crowed. Wade found his shirts mended without being asked, and the clock in the hall wound again, and a jar of wildflowers on a table where nothing had stood for three years."
- **MOLLY** (сидит на заборе, пока Уэйд чинит жердь): "How come you never smile, Mister Wade?"
- **WADE:** "Forgot how, I guess."
- **MOLLY:** "That's all right. I'll show you. It's easy."
- *Она растягивает щёки пальцами. Уэйд отворачивается — но камера видит: он улыбается.*
- **N02c:** "He taught her to milk the brown cow and to tell a hawk from a buzzard by the way it held its wings. She taught him the names of all four of her dolls, three of which she had left behind in Leadville. He never asked why they had left in such a hurry. Some questions, Wade knew, you only ask when you are ready to hear the answer."

### СЦЕНА 3. Город
*Яркий день, главная улица Сидар-Блаффа, лавка Хоббса.*

- **N04:** "On the fourth day Wade hitched the wagon and drove them into Cedar Bluff. The girl needed shoes. The baby needed milk. And Wade needed to know who sends three men through a storm after a widow with a baby."
- В лавке. Молли не отрывает глаз от новых башмачков на полке.
- **ELLIE:** "The small boots, please. I can pay half now and…"
- **HOBBS:** "No credit to strangers, ma'am. Cash or nothing."
- *Миссис Уиткомб у прилавка, через лорнет.*
- **MRS. WHITCOMB:** "A widow, sleeping under a widower's roof. In my day we had a word for women like that."
- *Тишина в лавке. Элли краснеет, прижимает младенца. Уэйд кладёт на прилавок двадцатидолларовую золотую монету.*
- **WADE:** "Boots for the girl. A sack of flour. And whatever else the lady needs."
- *Поворачивается к Уиткомб.*
- **WADE:** "In my day, Mrs. Whitcomb, we had a word for women who walk forty miles through a storm to keep their children. We called them mothers."
- *Кто-то в лавке хмыкает. Уиткомб багровеет и выходит. Молли прижимает к груди новые башмаки.*
- **MOLLY** (шёпотом): "Mama… he does bite."
- На улице Клара Мерсер (из первых фильмов) догоняет Элли с узелком: детская одежда.
- **CLARA:** "Lily's outgrown these. And Mrs. Price — don't mind Edna Whitcomb. She said worse about me."
- **N04c (дорога домой, закат, Молли спит в фургоне, прижав к себе башмаки):** "They drove home with the sun going down behind the ridge. Molly fell asleep in the back of the wagon with the new boots in her arms, still in their paper. Ellie didn't say a word for three miles. Then, without looking at him, she said, 'Nobody has stood up for me in a very long time, Wade.' He didn't answer. He didn't know how. But he drove a little slower the rest of the way."

### СЦЕНА 4. Телеграмма
*Контора маршала.*

- **MARSHAL HART:** "Wade. This came from Leadville yesterday."
- *Телеграмма крупно: «WOMAN, GIRL 7, INFANT BOY. ABDUCTED VANCE HEIR. REWARD $500. — GREER, ATTORNEY».*
- **MARSHAL:** "Lucius Vance. Silver king. The baby's his grandson, they say."
- **WADE:** "They say she abducted her own son?"
- **MARSHAL:** "Papers say the court gave the boy to the old man. Papers say she's unfit." (пауза) "I've never seen papers that could tell me who a mother is, Wade."
- **N05:** "Lucius Vance owned half the silver in Leadville and every judge who mattered. His only son, Henry, had died under a fallen beam in a shaft his father refused to brace. Henry left behind a wife the old man never wanted, a daughter he never visited, and a son. A boy was something Lucius Vance could use."

### СЦЕНА 4б. Салун (~1:00)
*Тот же вечер. Сидар-Блафф, салун, ливень за дверью. Пайк у стойки, вода стекает с пыльника. (Ливень смыл следы у ручья — три дня Пайк искал, кто заговорит.)*

- **PIKE:** "Woman with a baby. Red hair. Little girl. Came through in the rain."
- **BARTENDER:** "Lot of folks come through in the rain, mister."
- *Пайк кладёт на стойку золотую монету.*
- **PIKE:** "Five hundred dollars says somebody remembers."
- *Тишина. Из угла, где пьёт чай у окна, поднимается миссис Уиткомб.*
- **MRS. WHITCOMB:** "Try the Harlan place. Up the valley road. You can't miss it." (поджимает губы) "It's the one with the lamp in the window."
- **N05b:** "Edna Whitcomb would tell herself afterwards that she had only been doing her Christian duty. Nobody in Cedar Bluff ever quite believed her."

### СЦЕНА 5. Правда
*Вечер, крыльцо ранчо, закат, первые дальние зарницы.*

- **WADE:** "Henry Vance. Your husband."
- **ELLIE** (не сразу): "His father wants Sam. Not me. Not Molly. He had a doctor sign a paper that says I'm not right in the head. There's a place in Denver for women like that, Mr. Harlan. They don't come out."
- **WADE:** "So you walked."
- **ELLIE:** "Forty miles. Molly carried the bag the last ten."
- *Ночью гроза возвращается. Элли тихо выходит с детьми к воротам. Уэйд ждёт у ворот с фонарём.*
- **WADE:** "You're not walking out into the rain again, Ellie."
- **ELLIE:** "They'll burn your house down to get him."
- **WADE:** "Then they'll have to come through the gate first."
- **N06:** "He didn't tell her what he had been before he was a rancher. He didn't tell her about the badge in the bottom of his trunk, or the name men in Texas still said quietly. He only took the lantern from her hand and walked her back to the house."

- *Поздно ночью Уэйд открывает старый сундук. Под одеждой — серебряная звезда в круге и кольт в промасленной тряпке.*
- **N06b:** "Before Colorado there had been Texas. Eleven years with Company D of the Frontier Battalion, from the Rio Grande to the Staked Plains. He had ridden after cattle thieves and murderers and men who burned homesteads for sport, and he had brought most of them back. When Anna married him, she asked for one thing: that he put the badge away for good. He had kept that promise for nine years. He looked at it a long time that night. Then he loaded the Colt, and put the badge back in the trunk."

- *Детская. Молли на коленях у кровати, Уэйд замер в дверях.*
- **MOLLY:** "…and God bless Mama, and Sam, and Mister Wade, even though he doesn't smile much. And please make the rain stop." (подумав) "But not too fast."
- **N06c:** "The rain did not stop. It came back harder the next afternoon, black clouds rolling over the ridge from the west. And with it, down the valley road, came a closed buggy and three riders in long slickers."
- **N06d:** "Wade knew the man in the black hat by reputation. In Texas they had called him Deacon, because he liked to quote scripture over the men he shot. Wade had put his younger brother in Huntsville prison in the summer of '72. A man like Deacon Pike did not forget a thing like that. And neither did Wade."

### СЦЕНА 6. Гости у ворот
*Следующий день, после полудня, небо темнеет, начинается дождь. У ворот — бричка Грира и трое всадников.*

- **GREER** (из брички, раскрывая папку): "Good afternoon. Thaddeus Greer, attorney for Mr. Lucius Vance. I have a court order for the return of the child Samuel Vance. You'll hand him over, and we'll trouble you no further, Mister…" (смотрит в бумагу) "…Harlan."
- *Пайк, услышав имя, медленно поднимает голову.*
- **PIKE:** "Harlan. Wade Harlan." (пауза) "Captain Harlan. Company D. Texas Rangers."
- **WADE:** "That was a long time ago."
- **PIKE:** "You sent my brother to Huntsville. He died there."
- **WADE:** "Then you know how this ends, Deacon."
- *Грир нервно поправляет очки. Гроза. Пайк соскальзывает с седла.*
- **ELLIE** (в доме, Молли): "Take Sam up to the loft. Don't come down. Whatever you hear."
- *Молли карабкается с братом на сеновал и кладёт его в колыбель Дэниела.* (ключевой кадр)
- **Схватка под дождём (экшен, ~40 с):** выстрел сбивает шляпу Уэйда; один наёмник падает в грязь; второй прорывается к сараю — Элли встречает его на пороге с вилами; Пайк и Уэйд у ворот, в упор.
- *Топот: из пелены дождя вылетают Мерсер и маршал Харт.*
- **MERCER:** "Drop it, Pike!"
- *Пайк медлит — и роняет револьвер в грязь.*

### СЦЕНА 7. Бумаги
*Кухня, лампа. Грир мокрый, без шляпы.*

- **MARSHAL** (читая ордер): "Signed by Judge Amos Kittredge, Lake County. April 9th." (поднимает глаза) "Judge Kittredge was buried on April 2nd. I was at the funeral."
- *Грир бледнеет.*
- **ELLIE** достаёт из потёртой Библии сложенный лист.
- **ELLIE:** "Henry wrote this the night before he went down that shaft. He knew."
- **MARSHAL** (читает): "'Should anything befall me, my children are to remain with their mother, and with no one else. My father is not to raise them as he raised me.' Witnessed and signed."
- **N07:** "Within the month, Thaddeus Greer was disbarred in three states. Deacon Pike went to Cañon City for twelve years. And Lucius Vance, who had bought every judge in Lake County, found that a dead son's letter was the one thing his money could not buy back."

- **N07b:** "In Cedar Bluff, the story went around the way stories do. By Sunday, half the town had heard that Wade Harlan stood at his gate in the rain against three hired guns. The other half had heard about the boots. Mr. Hobbs sent a sack of sugar out to the ranch with his compliments, and did not send a bill."

### СЦЕНА 8. После грозы
*Утро, ослепительное солнце, мокрая трава в искрах.*

- *Уэйд впервые за три года поднимается по лестнице на сеновал. Снимает мешковину. Спускает колыбель вниз, несёт в дом и ставит у кровати, где спит Сэм.*
- **ELLIE** (в дверях): "Mr. Harlan… you don't have to."
- **WADE:** "It's waited long enough."
- *Молли на ступеньках крыльца, в новых башмаках.*
- **MOLLY:** "Mister Wade? Can we stay till the rain stops?"
- **WADE** (смотрит на ясное небо): "Rain's stopped, Molly."
- *Молли опускает голову. Пауза.*
- **WADE:** "Stay anyway."
- *Молли кидается ему на шею. Элли отворачивается, вытирая глаза.*

### ФИНАЛ
- **N08:** "They were married in June, in the little white church on Main Street, with Molly in her new boots throwing wildflowers at everyone, including the preacher. Mrs. Whitcomb was not invited. Nobody missed her. And on stormy nights, from then on, the lamp in the Harlan window stayed lit till morning — in case anyone else came knocking."
- **N08b (последний кадр, лампа в окне):** "Sam Harlan — he took Wade's name the year he turned six — grew up to wear a silver star of his own. People who knew him said he never once turned away a stranger at the door. When they asked him why, he said his father had taught him. ## Шортсы (снимаем вертикально сразу, в той же партии)
1. **«Only on Sundays»** — стук в грозу, «May we sleep in your barn?» → «No, ma'am… You'll sleep in the house.» → «Does he bite?» → «Only on Sundays.»
2. **«We called them mothers»** — лавка, сплетница, монета на прилавке, ответ Уэйда.
3. **«Captain Harlan»** — у ворот: «Captain Harlan. Company D. Texas Rangers.»
4. **«Stay anyway»** — колыбель и последняя реплика.
