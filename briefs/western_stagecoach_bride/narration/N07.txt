Rose slept fourteen hours in Clara's bed. When she woke, there was a faded green gingham dress folded on the chair, and the smell of bacon, and a little girl sitting at the foot of the bed with a rag doll, staring at her.

"Are you the bride?" Lily asked.

"I was supposed to be," said Rose.

They tried to open the box that afternoon. The brass key from Amos fit the padlock. But inside the box was a second lock, a small steel one, and no key in the world that any of them had.

Mercer turned the box over in his hands for a long time. Gold, he said, would have rattled.
