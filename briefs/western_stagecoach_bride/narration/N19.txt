Amos Pruitt lived. He testified in Denver in November with his arm in a sling and his spectacles mended with wire, and he shook Rose's hand in front of the whole courtroom.

Silas Crane went to the penitentiary at Cañon City. Buck Tolliver went with him.

And on a golden Sunday afternoon in Cedar Bluff, in front of the burned-out marshal's office, with half the town watching, Deputy Caleb Ward got down on one knee in the dirt.
