The news reached Cedar Bluff at sundown. The stage robbed. The driver alive. The old guard, Amos Pruitt, found at the way station with a bullet in his side, too weak to talk.

And the bride, gone.

Caleb Ward was in the saddle before the marshal finished reading the telegram. But it was John Mercer who found her. He had tracked men and cattle across half the West, and a woman in a lace dress leaves a trail like a lantern.
