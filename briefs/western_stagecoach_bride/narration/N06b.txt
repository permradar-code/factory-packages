Caleb searched the pass all night with a lantern, calling her name into the dark. A name he had written forty-nine times and never once said out loud.

He found the stagecoach on its side at the edge of the drop, the lead horse dead in the traces, and a scrap of ivory lace on the step. He did not find her.

At dawn the marshal made him come down the mountain to sleep. He didn't sleep.
